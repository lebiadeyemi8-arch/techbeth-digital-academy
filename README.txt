TECHBETH DIGITAL ACADEMY WEBSITE
================================

FILES
- index.html: Main responsive academy website and application form.
- success.html: Confirmation page with the supplied WhatsApp enrolment link.
- styles.css: Responsive visual design.
- script.js: Mobile navigation and programme pre-selection.

IMPORTANT: LIVE FORM SUBMISSIONS
The application form is configured for Netlify Forms (data-netlify="true"). It will collect and store submissions in the site's Netlify dashboard after the site is deployed to Netlify with form detection enabled. The static files alone do not collect applications while sitting on a phone or computer.

PUBLISHING STEPS
1. Create/sign in to a Netlify account.
2. In Netlify, choose Add new site > Deploy manually (or drag-and-drop deployment).
3. Upload the contents of this folder (index.html, success.html, styles.css, script.js) or upload the ZIP after extracting it.
4. After deployment, open the site's Forms area and confirm that the form named "techbeth-application" is detected. If form detection is disabled, enable it and redeploy.
5. Test by submitting a sample application. Confirm it appears in Netlify Forms and that the success page's WhatsApp button opens the supplied enrolment group.
6. Set a custom domain later if desired.

BEFORE ACCEPTING REAL STUDENT APPLICATIONS
- Add an official academy email and contact details to the website.
- Publish a clear privacy notice and decide who can access submissions.
- Configure notification emails in Netlify Forms.
- Test the full application flow on a phone and desktop.
- Do not collect payment card details or sensitive identity information through this form.

CURRENT FORM FIELDS
Full name, email, country, WhatsApp number, programme, experience level, learning goal and consent.

PROGRAMME LIST
Frontend Development; Backend Development; UI/UX Design; Graphic Design; AI Video Creation & AI Automation.

The website does not currently process payments, grant admission automatically, or add applicants to private classroom groups. The WhatsApp link is the public enrolment/information group supplied by the academy owner.
