PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

This notice explains how information submitted through the TECHBETH DIGITAL ACADEMY application form is intended to be used.

Information collected

The application form asks for your name, email address, country, WhatsApp number, programme interest, experience level and optional learning goals.

How information is used

Information is used to review your enquiry, contact you about the programme you selected, share enrolment updates and administer learning opportunities.

Storage and access

Applications submitted through the hosted form are stored by the website's form-hosting provider and may be accessed by authorised TECHBETH administrators for the purposes described above. The academy should restrict access to people who need it and delete information when it is no longer required.

Children and young learners

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to review this notice and help you submit the application. Do not submit information that is not needed for your enquiry.

Your choices

You may ask the academy to correct or delete information you submitted. Contact the academy through its official contact channel or enrolment group. An official privacy contact should be added by the academy before public launch.

Updates

This is an initial privacy notice and should be reviewed and updated by the academy before accepting applications at scale, including adding its official contact details and any applicable legal information.
