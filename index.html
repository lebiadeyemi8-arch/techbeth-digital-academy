05 — GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently
asked questions.

Can I apply from outside Nigeria?＋

Yes. The application form is designed for learners in different countries. Include your country and WhatsApp country code.

Do I need previous experience?＋

Many programmes welcome beginners. Choose the experience level that best describes you so the academy can guide you.

Does submitting the form mean I have been admitted?＋

No. It records your interest. The academy will share enrolment instructions and confirm the next steps separately.

How do I join the learning classes?＋

After application, join the enrolment information group. Classroom access is provided after the academy confirms your enrolment.
